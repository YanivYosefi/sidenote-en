No account, no server

Everything stays on your machine

Nothing to sign up for, no personal URL to keep safe. What you learned sits in one file on your own disk, and I have no way to read it.

~/.sidenote/state.json
{
  "target": "en",
  "level":  "B2",
  "learned": [
    { "expression": "to give someone a heads-up",
      "on": "2026-09-24" },
    { "expression": "to get it out of the way",
      "on": "2026-09-27" }
  ]
}

Fair questions

Does it slow me down?

No. The answer you asked for stays complete and comes first. The expression follows it, in one line.

Do I need an account?

None. No signup, no email, no personal URL. Install and start.

What does it cost?

Nothing. The plugin runs on the Claude subscription you already have.

Where is my progress stored?

In one file on your own machine, at ~/.sidenote/state.json. Nothing leaves it.

Which languages?

Any language Claude knows. Spanish, French, Italian, German, Portuguese, Japanese, Hebrew, Arabic, Russian and more.

How do I remove it completely?

claude plugin uninstall sidenote, then delete the folder. That is all.

I stopped for a month. What happens?

Nothing is lost. The file stays where it is and the expressions wait. They do not pile up into a debt of two hundred reviews waiting for you on the day you come back. You pick up where you left off.
